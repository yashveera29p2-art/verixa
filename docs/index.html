Add it to your home screen

  1. Tap Open Verixa above (in Safari).
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of Safari (on newer iPhones it may be under ⋯).
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Open Verixa from its new icon from now on.

Opening from the home-screen icon gives the full-screen app and keeps you signed in.

Download Verixa
About 21 MB · Android only · always the latest version
⬇️ Downloading Verixa.apk… When it finishes, tap Open.

Install in 3 taps

  1. Tap Download Verixa. If your browser asks, tap Download (or Download anyway).
  2. When it finishes, tap Open in the download message, or tap the download notification.
  3. Tap Install (or Update), then Open. Verixa is now on your home screen.

First time? If Android says installs from this source aren’t allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.

If Play Protect says the app is unknown, tap More details → Install anyway. Android shows this for every app that isn’t from the Play Store.

Very old phone and it says “App not installed”? Use the version for older phones instead.

Can’t find the file? Open your Files (or My Files) app → Downloads → Verixa.apk.
